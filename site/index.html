Three stages. One learning loop.

From scattered information to confident contribution.

01 / SOURCE

Start with what exists.

Bring together the knowledge already living in your repositories, documentation, and team wikis.

Today: local repositories, Markdown, JavaScript/TypeScript, and Go.
Planned: wiki and other knowledge-source connectors.

02 / GENERATE

Make the context actionable.

Generate focused, contextualized knowledge snippets that explain how a project works and build familiarity with its concepts, decisions, and code.

Today: source-attributed cards, quality filtering, and optional AI generation through a configured endpoint.

03 / LEARN

Build understanding every day.

Learn through regular, gamified spaced repetition woven into your daily workflow—with a path to Teams, GitHub, and Slack integrations.

Today: a local Teams-style review UI and spaced-repetition engine.
Planned: native Teams, GitHub, and Slack delivery integrations.

Your project. Your learning loop.

Cards and review history live in your project's .flashlearn/ directory. Offline extraction stays local; enabling an AI endpoint sends selected code to that endpoint, subject to its permissions and retention policies.

The public demo contains only curated public samples. It never accesses your repository and never submits ratings to a backend.
